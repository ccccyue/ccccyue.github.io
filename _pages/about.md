I am a CRM–Laval Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Université Laval, under the supervision of Professors Marcu-Antone Orsoni, Damir Kinzebulatov, and Alexandre Girouard. I received my Ph.D. in June 2025 from Memorial University, where I worked with Professor Jie Xiao.

My research lies at the intersection of stochastic differential equations (SDEs), partial differential equations (PDEs), and harmonic analysis. My current research interests include:

- Non-local SDEs with critical drifts and local blow-ups.

- Kinetic SDEs and kinetic Fokker–Planck equations.

- Hyperbolic Riesz potentials.

- Non-local methods in image processing.
